Bright Cove

You never know what you'll find in tide pools - there's always something amazing tucked away.

Bright Cove is a bioluminescent puzzle adventure set in the world's most secret coastal corners.

How It Works

1.Choose Your Cove
Every level's basically its own little tidal zone that we designed from scratch, complete with unique puzzles and backstory to discover.
2.Read the Tide
The tide cycle shifts every few moves — solve puzzles in rhythm with it to reach deeper, glowing layers below.
3.Restore the Light
Connect the glowing creatures to light up the whole area and unlock the next coastal region.

Features

  • Living Puzzles

    Every creature in the cove reacts to your moves — the ecosystem shifts as you solve, not just the grid.

  • Tide Cycle Mechanics

    Don't worry about the tide timer stressing you out - it's more about adding strategy than rushing you. Actually rewards taking your time.

  • Cove Cartography

    Unlock a hand-illustrated map of the coastline that fills in as you explore, bit by bit as you explore.

  • No Dead Ends

    Every puzzle has multiple valid paths — the game surfaces hints only when you want them.

About

Bright Cove was built for players who want to slow down without switching off. We've combined really satisfying puzzles with beautiful ocean visuals — something you can play for five minutes or an hour without losing the thread. No timers pushing you. No energy bars. Just the tide, the glow, and the next cove waiting.
